Helminth ability locks

A locked ability is never replaced by a subsume. Unlocked slots are fair game; the solver picks at most one subsume, at diminished Helminth values.

Owner stats (for Link mods)

Link mods scale off your Warframe's stats. Leave blank and Link mods contribute 0 (the result will say so).

Locked pieces

A locked mod must appear in the build — the solver chooses its slot and may only trim its rank to fit capacity (it will say so). Locks are how you force in mods the stat model doesn't score, like Primed Sure Footed. The search covers the full pool for this category, including stat-less mods.

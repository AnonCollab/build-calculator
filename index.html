Routes

Augments are mechanic mods — the solver won't pick them for stats; turn on Ability augments to lock the ones your build uses. An augment appears in the pickers only for a frame that has its ability (natively, or subsumed in via Helminth).

Prefer spending Forma (up to your budget) over Archon Shards for stat targets.

Upkeep excludes event-triggered effects with windows ≤ 6s (locks override). Hybrid keeps that discipline but lets the solver spend the allowance on the highest-value burst pieces (locked burst pieces never consume it).

Conditional buffs (procced / stacking sources) count toward stats only when that toggle is on. Legacy Arcane Helmets default to off — they only apply when the selected frame has one.

Helminth subsume choice

Let the solver pick the subsume, or pin the exact donor ability and the ability it replaces. A non-modelled donor (most of them) rides as a build instruction with its diminished text — it never moves paper stats.

Helminth ability locks

A locked ability is never replaced by a subsume. Unlocked slots are fair game; the solver picks at most one subsume, at diminished Helminth values.

Focus school

One school per build, picked like a Helminth choice. The school's stat-modelled nodes apply as build pieces — conditional ones count only when Conditional buffs is on and obey build intent (burst actives are pruned under Upkeep). Its other nodes are mechanic or Operator effects: they are listed in the results notes, never invented as stats.

Weapon arcanes and conditional (procced / stacking) effects count only under their toggles. The stance slot is capacity-only.

Routes

Amps have no mods, shards or Forma — Calculate solves Operator/Amp arcanes only.

Owner stats (for Link mods)

Link mods scale off your Warframe's stats. Leave blank and Link mods contribute 0 (the result will say so).

Included & excluded pieces

A locked (included) mod must appear in the build — the solver chooses its slot and may only trim its rank to fit capacity (it will say so). Locks are how you force in mods the stat model doesn't score, like Primed Sure Footed. An excluded (banned) mod or arcane is never used, by the search or the fill phase. The search covers the full pool for this category, including stat-less mods. A piece cannot be both locked and excluded.

              Forma

              One Omni Forma makes the Aura slot match any Aura polarity (full doubled capacity bonus). A yes/no choice — the slot can also be Forma'd to a specific polarity from the board instead.

              Starting Pool —

              The polarities already invested in this item (its regular slots + Exilus slot). The solver rearranges them for free and only plans new Forma beyond them. The Aura slot (and a melee's Stance slot) keep their own selectors — they are never part of the pool.

              Off by default. When on, the solver may convert a Starting Pool polarity into a different one — only when every other slot is already polarized and the build is otherwise unreachable. Each conversion costs +1 Forma and counts against the Forma budget; free rearrangement and new Forma on blank slots are always preferred.
